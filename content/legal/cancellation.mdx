If you are a consumer in the UK or the EU, you can cancel a new package within 14 days, as explained in [section 4A of our Terms](/legal/terms#4a-consumers-your-right-to-cancel). You don't have to use this form: any clear message works, for example an email or a support ticket.

## Model cancellation form

Copy the text below into an email to <a href={"mailto:" + site.contact.email + "?subject=Cancellation"}>{site.contact.email}</a>, fill it in and send it.

> To {legalEntity}, {site.contact.email}:
>
> I hereby give notice that I cancel my contract for the following service:
>
> Package and site(s): …
>
> Ordered on: …
>
> Name: …
>
> Email address used for my account: …
>
> Date: …

## What happens next

We confirm by email that we received your cancellation, and refund you within 14 days by the same method you used to pay. If you asked us to start straight away, we deduct a proportionate amount for the time the service ran before you told us.
