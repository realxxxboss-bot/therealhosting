This page explains what we commit to. We do not publish an uptime percentage yet: we will only publish one when we have real measurements to back it up. Live service health is on our [status page](/status).

## What we do for every site

- **Monitoring** from outside our own network, checking your site regularly.
- **Alerts** to our team when a site goes down, and to you by email (and WhatsApp when available).
- **Investigation** by a person as soon as an alert is raised during support hours.
- **Incident notes**: after any outage, we tell you what happened and what we changed.

## Support response targets

| Priority | Example | First response target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Urgent | Site down, data loss | As fast as possible, within support hours |
| High | Feature broken, slow site | Same business day |
| Normal | Questions, changes | Within one business day |

Support hours: {site.contact.supportHours}.

## Maintenance

Planned maintenance is announced in your dashboard at least 48 hours in advance. We schedule it at low-traffic times and avoid downtime wherever possible.

## Backups

Daily off-site backups are kept for the number of days in your plan. Restores are done by our team on request.

## Credits

If a problem caused by us takes your site offline for an extended time, contact support. We will review it with you and offer a fair service credit.
