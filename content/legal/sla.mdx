This page explains what we commit to. We do not publish an uptime percentage yet: we will only publish one when we have real measurements to back it up. Service health will be shown on our [status page](/status) once monitoring is connected.

## What we do for every site

- **Monitoring** from outside our own network, checking your site regularly.
- **Alerts** to us when a site goes down, and to you by email.
- **Investigation** by a person, as soon as we see an alert.
- **Incident notes**: after any outage, we tell you what happened and what we changed.

## Support response targets

We answer support every day, including weekends.

| Priority | Example | First response target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Urgent | Site down, data at risk | As soon as we see it, and within 24 hours at most |
| High | Something broken, slow site | Within 24 hours |
| Normal | Questions, changes | Within 24 hours |

These are targets, not guarantees. Support hours: {site.contact.supportHours}.

## Maintenance

Planned maintenance is announced on our status page and by email at least 48 hours in advance. We schedule it at low-traffic times and plan it to avoid downtime.

## Backups

Daily off-site backups are kept for the number of days in your plan. Restores are done by us on request.

## Outages

If a problem caused by us takes your site offline for an extended time, contact support. We will review it with you, explain what happened, and agree a fair credit against your next invoice. This does not limit your rights under our [Terms](/legal/terms).
