To keep our network safe and fair for everyone, you must not use {site.name} to host, send or do any of the following.

## Not allowed

- Anything illegal where you or your visitors are, or where our servers are ({site.dataRegions.join(", ")}).
- Malware, phishing pages, or anything designed to steal credentials or harm devices.
- Spam or unsolicited bulk email.
- Content that infringes someone else's copyright or trademark.
- Content that sexually exploits children, or promotes terrorism or violence.
- Attacks on other systems: scanning, denial of service, brute-force logins.
- Cryptocurrency mining, or running workloads unrelated to your websites.
- Open proxies or VPN endpoints.
- Reselling our Services without a written agreement.

## Fair use of resources

Each plan has reserved resources. If a site uses far more than its plan allows for a long time, we will contact you to discuss an upgrade or a fix. We will not cut you off without talking to you first, unless the site is harming other customers or our network.

## What we do if this policy is broken

We may remove content, block traffic or suspend a site to stop harm. Where we can, we will warn you first and explain why.
