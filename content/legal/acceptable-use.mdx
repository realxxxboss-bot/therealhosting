To keep our network safe and fair for everyone, you must not use {site.name} to host, send or do any of the following.

## Not allowed

- Anything illegal where you or your visitors are, or where our servers are ({site.dataRegions.join(", ")}).
- Malware, phishing pages, or anything designed to steal credentials or harm devices.
- Spam or unsolicited bulk email.
- Content that infringes someone else's copyright or trademark.
- Content that sexually exploits children, or promotes terrorism or violence.
- Attacks on other systems: scanning, denial of service, brute-force logins.
- Cryptocurrency mining, or running workloads unrelated to your websites.
- Open proxies or VPN endpoints.
- Reselling our Services without a written agreement.

## Fair use of resources

Each plan has its own CPU, memory and storage limits. If a site uses far more than its plan allows for a long time, we will contact you to discuss an upgrade or a fix. We will not cut you off without talking to you first, unless the site is harming other customers or our network.

## What we do if this policy is broken

We may remove content, block traffic or suspend a site to stop harm. Where we can, we will warn you first and explain why.

## Reporting abuse or illegal content

Email <a href={"mailto:" + site.contact.email}>{site.contact.email}</a> with "Abuse" in the subject. To help us act quickly, include:

- the exact web address (URL) of the content;
- what is wrong with it, and why you believe it is illegal or breaks this policy;
- for copyright or trademark complaints, the work or mark concerned and confirmation that you own it or act for the owner;
- your name and email, so we can reply (you may report anonymously, but we may not be able to update you).

We acknowledge reports within 2 business days. Clearly illegal or harmful content (malware, phishing, child sexual abuse material) is disabled as soon as we confirm it. For other reports, we contact the customer, give them a chance to respond, and decide within a reasonable time.

## If your content is reported

We tell you what was reported and what we did, unless the law or a risk of harm stops us. If you think we got it wrong, reply with your reasons and we will review the decision. We restore content when the report turns out to be mistaken.
