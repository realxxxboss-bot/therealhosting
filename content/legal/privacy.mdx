## International transfers

Most data stays in the EU. Where a provider above processes data outside the UK or EU (for example, email sending), we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

## How long we keep data

- Account and billing records: as long as your account is active, then as long as tax law requires.
- Support tickets: up to 3 years after closing.
- Backups: as long as your plan says (7 to 30 days), then deleted automatically.
- Sign-in records: 12 months.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or move your personal data, and object to processing. Write to <a href={"mailto:" + site.contact.email}>{site.contact.email}</a>. You can also complain to your data protection authority, for example the UK ICO.

## Cookies

We use only what is strictly needed, so we do not show a cookie banner. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

| Name | Purpose | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `zf_session` | Keeps you signed in to the dashboard | Up to 7 days, or until you sign out |
| `zf_2fa` | Remembers the first sign-in step while you enter your two-factor code | 10 minutes |
| `theme` (browser storage, not a cookie) | Remembers whether you chose the light or dark theme | Until you clear it |

## Security

Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, and secrets such as two-factor keys are encrypted. Access to client data is limited to the staff who need it, and admin actions are recorded in an audit log. If a breach affects your personal data, we tell you and the relevant authority without undue delay.

## Children

Our Services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16.
