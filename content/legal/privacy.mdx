## International transfers

Most data stays in the EU. Where a provider above processes data outside the UK or EU (for example, email sending in the United States), we rely on appropriate safeguards: the provider's certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and its UK extension where it has one, or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses with the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

## How long we keep data

- **Account and billing records:** while your account is active, then for as long as tax law requires (usually 6 years).
- **Support tickets:** up to 3 years after they are closed.
- **Backups:** for the number of days in your plan (7 to 30), then deleted.
- **Sign-in records and audit log:** up to 12 months.
- **Contact form messages that don't lead to an account:** up to 12 months.

We review stored data regularly and delete what has passed these periods.

## Cookies

We use only what is strictly needed, so we do not show a cookie banner. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

| Name | Purpose | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `zf_session` | Keeps you signed in to the dashboard and remembers which account you are viewing | Up to 7 days, or until you sign out |

| `theme` (browser storage, not a cookie) | Remembers whether you chose the light or dark theme | Until you clear it |

## Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt and never stored in readable form. Two-factor keys are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Emailed links are stored only as hashes and expire. Admin accounts must use two-factor authentication, and admin actions are recorded in an audit log. If a breach affects your personal data, we tell you and the relevant authority without undue delay. More on our [security page](/security).
