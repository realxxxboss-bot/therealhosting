You are the controller of personal data in Your Content (for example, your website's visitor data, form submissions and customer records). {legalEntity} is your processor. If you act for another controller, we are your sub-processor and you confirm you are authorised to give us instructions.

## 2. What we process

## 3. Our obligations

We will:

1. process the data only on your documented instructions, which are these Terms, your use of the dashboard and your support requests, unless the law requires otherwise (in which case we tell you first, if allowed);
2. tell you if we believe an instruction breaks data protection law;
3. make sure everyone who can access the data is bound by confidentiality;
4. keep appropriate technical and organisational security measures in place (section 5);
5. help you, as far as reasonably possible, to answer requests from data subjects and to meet your obligations on security, breach notification, impact assessments and consultation with authorities;
6. make available the information needed to show we comply with this DPA, and allow for and contribute to audits (section 8).

## 4. Personal data breaches

We will tell you without undue delay, and in any case within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Your Content. We will give you the information we have, update you as we learn more, and take reasonable steps to contain it.

## 5. Security measures

Our current measures include: encryption in transit (TLS) for all traffic to the dashboard and to hosted sites; isolated containers per site with resource limits; daily off-site backups stored in the EU; access to production systems limited to the people who need it, with two-factor authentication required for admin accounts; an audit log of admin actions; passwords hashed with scrypt; and prompt security updates to the hosting environment. See our [security page](/security). We may improve these measures; we will not reduce their overall level.

We host Your Content in the EU. Where a sub-processor processes it outside the UK or EU, we use a lawful transfer mechanism: an adequacy decision (including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and its UK extension where the provider is certified), or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses with the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

## 8. Audits

On request, we will answer reasonable written questions about our compliance, at no charge, once a year (or more often after a breach or if an authority requires it). Where that is not enough, you may arrange an audit with at least 30 days' notice, during business hours, at your cost, by an auditor bound by confidentiality, without access to other customers' data.

When the Services end, you can take a copy of your files and databases for 30 days. After that we delete Your Content from our systems; copies in backups are deleted as the backups expire. We keep data only where the law requires us to, and continue to protect it while we do.
