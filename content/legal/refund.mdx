## Consumers: 14 days to cancel

If you are a consumer in the UK or the EU, you can cancel a new package within 14 days for any reason. This is set out in [section 4A of our Terms](/legal/terms#4a-consumers-your-right-to-cancel), and you can use our [cancellation form](/legal/cancellation). It applies on top of everything below.

## Monthly packages

Cancel any time. Your package stays active until the end of the month you have paid for. We do not refund partial months.

## Yearly packages

- If you cancel within **{site.refundWindowDays} days** of your first yearly payment, we refund the full amount.
- After that, we refund the yearly price minus the monthly price for each month that has started. If that is zero or less, there is no refund. For example, if you cancel during your 4th month, we refund the yearly price minus 4 × the monthly price.
- When a pro-rata refund is paid, the package ends on the day you cancel.

## Migrations

Migrations are free, so there is nothing to refund.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back by the same method you used to pay (bank transfer or Wise), in USD. We aim to send refunds within 10 business days, and within 14 days for a consumer cancellation. We don't charge a fee for refunds; your own bank may charge for receiving an international transfer.

Open a ticket in your dashboard or email <a href={"mailto:" + site.contact.email}>{site.contact.email}</a>. A person reads every request.
