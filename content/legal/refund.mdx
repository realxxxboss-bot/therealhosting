## Monthly packages

Cancel any time. Your package stays active until the end of the month you have paid for. We do not refund partial months.

## Yearly packages

- If you cancel within **{site.refundWindowDays} days** of your first yearly payment, we refund the full amount.
- After that, we refund the unused whole months, minus any months used, at the monthly price. For example, if you cancel after 3 months, we refund the yearly price minus 3 × the monthly price.

## Migrations

Migrations are free, so there is nothing to refund.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back by the same method you used to pay (bank transfer or Wise), in USD. Bank or transfer fees may apply. We aim to send refunds within 10 business days.

## How to ask

Open a ticket in your dashboard or email <a href={"mailto:" + site.contact.supportEmail}>{site.contact.supportEmail}</a>.
