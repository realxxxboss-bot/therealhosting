We would rather hear about a problem than have you put up with it. This is how to complain and what we do.

## How to complain

Email <a href={"mailto:" + site.contact.email + "?subject=Complaint"}>{site.contact.email}</a> with "Complaint" in the subject, or open a ticket in your dashboard and say it is a complaint. Tell us what happened, what you expected, and what would put it right.

## What we do

1. **Acknowledge** your complaint within 2 business days, and tell you who is handling it.
2. **Look into it**, including logs and the history of your account, and ask you questions if we need to.
3. **Reply in full within 14 days**, with what we found, what we will do, and any refund or credit. If it takes longer, we tell you why and when you will hear from us.

## If you are still not satisfied

Reply to our answer and ask for it to be reviewed. Someone who wasn't involved in the first answer, where that is possible, looks at it again and replies within 14 days.

If you are a consumer and we cannot agree, you may also contact a consumer advice service, such as Citizens Advice in the UK or the European Consumer Centre in your country, or go to court as set out in [section 19 of our Terms](/legal/terms#19-governing-law-and-disputes).

## Data protection complaints

You can also complain to a data protection authority, as explained in our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy#your-rights).
