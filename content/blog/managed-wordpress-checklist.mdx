"Managed WordPress" can mean almost anything. Here is what we think it should mean, so you can compare hosts on substance.

## Updates that someone checks

Automatic updates are easy. **Checked** updates are the point: a backup first, the update, then a look at the important pages. If something breaks, a rollback.

## Backups you can restore

Ask three questions: Where are backups stored? How many days are kept? Who restores them, and how fast? A backup on the same server as the site is not enough.

## Security at the edge

A firewall and login protection should stop most attacks before WordPress sees them. Malware scans should run on a schedule, and someone should act on the results.

## Caching that fits WordPress

Page caching for visitors, object caching for logged-in users and shops, and the right exceptions for carts and checkouts.

## A person who answers

When the site is down, you want a human who can see the server, not a script that asks you to clear your browser cache.
